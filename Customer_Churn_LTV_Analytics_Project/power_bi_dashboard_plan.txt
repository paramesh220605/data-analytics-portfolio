CUSTOMER RETENTION, CHURN & LTV ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
Power BI / Excel Dashboard Specification

Page 1 – Executive Overview
KPI Cards:
1. Total Customers
2. Churned Customers
3. Churn Rate %
4. Retention Rate %
5. Average LTV
6. Total Customer Value

Charts:
- Donut: Churn vs Retained
- Column: Churn Rate by Contract Type
- Column: Average LTV by Contract Type
- Bar: Churn Rate by Satisfaction Score
- Line/Column: Average LTV by Tenure Band

Slicers:
- Gender
- Contract Type
- Payment Method
- Risk Category
- Churn

Page 2 – Customer Risk Analysis
- Table: Customer ID, Contract, Satisfaction, Support Tickets,
  Login Frequency, Monthly Charges, LTV, Churn, Risk Category
- Conditional formatting on Risk Category and Churn
- Scatter chart: Monthly Charges vs LTV, legend = Churn
- Bar chart: High-risk customers by contract type

Recommended Power BI calculated measures:
Total Customers = DISTINCTCOUNT(Data[Customer_ID])
Churned Customers = CALCULATE([Total Customers], Data[Churn] = "Yes")
Churn Rate % = DIVIDE([Churned Customers], [Total Customers], 0)
Retained Customers = CALCULATE([Total Customers], Data[Churn] = "No")
Retention Rate % = DIVIDE([Retained Customers], [Total Customers], 0)
Average LTV = AVERAGE(Data[Simple_LTV])
Total Customer Value = SUM(Data[Simple_LTV])

Business goal:
Identify customers with high churn probability and high lifetime value,
then prioritize retention campaigns.
